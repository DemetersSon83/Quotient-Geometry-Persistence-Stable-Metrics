This repository packages the computational material accompanying
Quotient Geometry and Persistence-Stable Metrics for Swarm Configurations
by Mark M. Bailey, integrated manuscript revision of 3 October 2026.

The original study scripts and reference outputs are preserved byte-for-byte.
provenance.json identifies their source archive and SHA-256 hashes.
The isolated runner, repository documentation, and CI configuration were added
when packaging the study on 5 October 2026.

No new license grant is made by this packaging. A repository-wide license
should be selected by the rights holder before an open-source release.
No reviewer reports, correspondence, or Rocq formalization files are included.
